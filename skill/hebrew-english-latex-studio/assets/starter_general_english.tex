{\LARGE\sffamily\bfseries\noindent Proposed agenda\par}\medskip
The proposed meeting has three parts: a status review, discussion of open
questions, and selection of the next action. This structure gives participants
a starting point for preparing the information needed in the discussion.
\section{Context}
The discussion begins with a brief account of the current status and the
decision to be made. Missing information is recorded as an open question.
\section{Details}
Following the review, participants consider the alternatives and the questions
that require answers. The proposal is to record the decision at the end of the
discussion, together with responsibility for further work.
\section{Next step}
Before the meeting, each participant can submit a question or information to
consider. Afterward, a short record can state the decision and agreed action.
