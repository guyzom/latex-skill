{\LARGE\sffamily\bfseries\noindent Task handover\par}\medskip
A task handover connects the current status with the next action. A short
account of completed work and the outstanding decision gives the recipient
a clear starting point.
\section{The main idea}
The current status, responsibility for further work, and next action serve
different purposes. Additional detail belongs in the account when the recipient
needs it to carry out the action.
\section{Practical meaning}
Before the handover, confirm that the recipient understands the next action
and has the information needed to begin. Record any open question beside that
action.
