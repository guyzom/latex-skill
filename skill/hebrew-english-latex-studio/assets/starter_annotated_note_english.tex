{\LARGE\sffamily\bfseries\noindent Comparing values\par}\medskip
Comparing two values requires a common definition of the measured quantity and
its units\footnote{For example, one metre equals $100$ centimetres. Different
units can describe the same length.}.
\section{Claim and assumptions}
Assume that the values $x$ and $y$ can be compared directly\DocNoteMark{scope}.
The difference $d=x-y$ describes the size and direction of their separation.
% DeclareDocNote captures the note outside the prose; PrintDocNotes emits it here.
\PrintDocNotes
\section{Implication}
Under this assumption\DocNoteMark{scope}, a positive difference means $x>y$,
a negative difference means $x<y$, and $d=0$ means that the values are
equal\footnote{A difference describes a gap between values; it does not explain
the cause of that gap.}.
